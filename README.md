## ✨ Features

- 🔍 Detects the client's goal, required skills, deadline and questions
- 🔑 Spots hidden keywords like *"start your proposal with the word…"*
- 🧠 Opens with **the client's problem**, not your bio (the first lines are what clients see)
- 📁 Adds your most relevant past projects and a clear 4-step plan
- ❓ Answers every question in the post in its own section
- 🎚️ 3 tones: friendly, formal, short
- 💾 Your profile is saved in the browser; live character counter

## 🚀 Why I built it

Generic proposals get ignored. This gives you a strong, specific first draft so you can spend your time on the details that win the job.

## 🛠️ How to use

1. Open the **[live demo](https://aliakbarrme.github.io/freelance-proposal-helper/)**
2. Fill in *About you* once
3. Paste a job post → **Generate** → edit and send

To run it locally, download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. No build step, no server.
